A fictional territorial network

After the Storm / Vale Norte

A severe windstorm hits a fictional municipality with a town centre, dispersed villages and disrupted roads, power and communications. Further adverse weather complicates the response.

Organisations and formal responsibilities existed before the event. The response activates those arrangements and integrates new contributions. As immediate operations reduce, some tasks end while others require handover into continuing support and recovery.

Formal command and emergent contributions are both part of the story. Neither hierarchy nor spontaneity guarantees functional capacity.

All participants, episodes and outcomes are invented. The eight focal roles are not a complete operational chart. National structures, crews, contractors and households extend beyond the visual. No particular Portuguese institution’s performance is assessed.

Episode interview guide

  1. What concrete function needed to be assured?
  2. What was known, uncertain or unavailable at the time?
  3. What happened first, then next?
  4. Who participated, through which formal or informal channels?
  5. Who could authorise the action? What means and specialist capacities were available?
  6. How did local information or civic contributions enter the task?
  7. What happened, for whom, and what remained unmet?
  8. When the task or response phase ended, who inherited the unfinished work?

A bounded first pilot

One municipal partnership; three priority functions. Reconstruct recent exercises or past events using authorised accounts from operational roles, civic organisations and residents.

Triangulate selected episodes with other participants and relevant records. Separate formal mandates from what reportedly happened. Collect counterexamples and keep uncertain outcomes explicit.

Deliverables: episode records, the same repertoire explorer, coordination choices, transition gaps and a bounded verification plan.

What the visual can and cannot establish

All case data are synthetic. Lines illustrate narrated coordination, not a measured network. Solid links can carry commands, information or contributions; read the excerpt to distinguish them. Dashed links mark pending commitments or blocked pathways. Faded actors are unmentioned, without proving absence. Shaded regions and positions have no analytical meaning.

A relational field is the proposed lens; episodes show illustrative configurations under particular demands. This does not prove the field ontology, validate a mathematical model or predict disaster performance. No topology is automatically effective for every task.

Responsible interpretation

In real work, agree access and disclosure with participating organisations and communities. Avoid publishing identifiable vulnerability lists or sensitive operational details. Retain constraints known at the time; do not infer incompetence from a poor outcome. AI may propose codes and retrieve passages, but must not invent contacts or outcomes.

This is a coordination demonstration, not an emergency procedure, command system or technical rescue guide.

Five-minute client conversation

A territory can mobilise.

0:00–0:45 / The problem

A list of agencies and volunteers does not explain whether a warning becomes readiness, assistance reaches a need or unfinished work survives the emergency surge.

0:45–1:30 / The proposition

Compare concrete episodes of preparation, sensemaking, rescue, allocation, continuity and recovery. The wider relational field activates different configurations for different functions.

1:30–3:00 / The product

Show “Rescue & protect”, E05/E06. A clear operational lead connects specialist roles. Then show “Mobilise & allocate”, E07/E08: civic contributions need a different arrangement. Open each source and its limits.

3:00–4:15 / The decision

Show “Maintain essential support” and “Recover & learn”. Immediate tasks stand down while continuing needs require resources, owners and review. Some relationships persist in a different configuration.

4:15–5:00 / The invitation

Commission a bounded pilot around three functions using authorised accounts and records. Choose one coordination change and verify it through an exercise or later observation.
